How to use this guide

The Witcher 3 is generous — until it isn't. A handful of story moments quietly close whole questlines, lock endings and remove Gwent cards for good. This guide shows you exactly where those moments are, so you can explore freely and never lose 100 hours of choices to one boat ride.

Chapter I

The Three Points of No Return

Almost everything in the game waits for you. These moments do not.

#MomentWhat it closes
1Leaving White Orchard
Riding with the Nilfgaardian escort to the Emperor		 Every open White Orchard side quest, contract and Gwent game.
2Sailing to the Isle of Mists
Main quest The Isle of Mists		 The biggest cutoff in the game. Around 19 side quests auto-fail, including Keira Metz's whole arc, the Triss romance, the quests that later unlock the plot against Radovid, and your last chance to recruit allies for Kaer Morhen.
3DLC one-way doors
Hearts of Stone auction · Blood and Wine fairy-tale land		 In Hearts of Stone, the auction and a later main quest each close a side quest (page 10). The Land of a Thousand Fables is visited only once — explore it fully before you leave.
Also watch out for
Some characters die or leave as the story moves on. Play them at Gwent and finish their errands while you can — and see page 9 for the seven cards with no second chance.

Save habits that save runs

  • Hard save before every main quest marked with a boat, a portal or a "this will take you away" warning.
  • Keep one save per region: White Orchard · Velen · Novigrad · Skellige · Kaer Morhen.
  • Before the Isle of Mists, make a manual save and never overwrite it — you'll thank yourself.

White Orchard Checklist

The tutorial region is small, but everything here closes when you ride out to meet the Emperor.

Side quests & contracts

  • Missing in Action
  • On Death's Bed
  • Precious Cargo
  • A Frying Pan, Spick and Span
  • Twisted Firestarter
  • Temerian Valuables
  • Contract: Devil by the Well

Before you leave

  • Play the innkeeper at Gwent and buy the cards on offer.
  • Read every notice board in the village.
  • Clear the "?" markers — easy early loot and XP.
  • Unlock every signpost (fast travel).
  • Visit the Places of Power you find — free skill points.
  • Check your inventory: sell junk, keep crafting diagrams.
Don't look for it now
Faithful Friend is the one White Orchard quest that doesn't fail when you leave — but it only appears in the epilogue of the Empress ending.

Chapter V · Spoilers

Ciri's Fate — The Five Moments

The game quietly scores how you treat Ciri in five moments across three late quests. The tally, plus one political condition, decides her ending.

QuestPositive choice
Blood on the BattlefieldCheer her up with the snowball fight ("Think I know what might lift your spirits.").
Blood on the Battlefield
only if you visit Emhyr		Refuse the Emperor's payment. Accepting it counts against you.
Final PreparationsLet Ciri speak to the Lodge of Sorceresses alone.
Child of the Elder BloodWhen she loses her temper in Avallac'h's laboratory, say "Go for it."
Child of the Elder BloodGo with her to Skjall's grave ("Yeah. I'll go with you.").

The three endings

Witcher Ciri
At least 3 positive moments, and don't take Ciri to see Emhyr. The most popular "best" ending.
Empress Ciri
At least 3 positive moments, take her to Emhyr, and make sure Nilfgaard wins the war (page 8 — Roche's side of Reason of State).
The bad ending
Fewer than 3 positive moments leads to the saddest ending. Accepting Emhyr's coin is the most common way players lose a point without noticing.
Tip
If you skip the Emhyr visit, only four moments are scored — so three of four must be positive.

How to unlock Reason of State
1) Finish A Deadly Plot (it needs A Matter of Life and Death and Now or Never). 2) In the main quest Blindingly Obvious, pay Dijkstra with information about the Emperor — don't fight him ("Tell you what the emperor's after if you let us go."). 3) Complete Reason of State before On Thin Ice — it fails after that.
Plan your whole run in one line
Want Empress Ciri? You need Now or Never → A Deadly Plot → Blindingly Obvious (info to Dijkstra) → Reason of State (kill Radovid) → side with Roche plus the Emhyr visit and 3 good moments with Ciri. Want Witcher Ciri? Skip Emhyr, keep 3 good moments — politics are free.

Chapter VII

Gwent — Cards You Can Lose Forever

The quest Collect 'Em All asks for 120 unique cards, and the game never tells you which ones you're missing. Patches added backups for many cards — but not for the seven on the right.

Card rules of thumb

  • Play every named Gwent player the moment you meet them.It's the safest habit — and needed for the card collection anyway.
  • Buy out merchant decks in Velen and Novigrad early.Cheaper and easier than hunting them later.
  • Play the innkeepers in every region.
  • Finish all Gwent side quests before the Isle of Mists.

7 cards with no second chance

  • A Matter of Life and DeathMasquerade: win Milva, Vampire: Bruxa and Dandelion from the three players.
  • High StakesWin all four matches for the four leader cards (Foltest, Emhyr, Francesca, Eredin). Don't leave the Passiflora mid-tournament — the quest fails.

Chapter VIII · Point of no return #3

Hearts of Stone & Blood and Wine

Both expansions have their own one-way doors. They're easy to miss because the game doesn't warn you.

Hearts of Stone

  • At the auction in Open Sesame! buy every lot.It's the only way to unlock the side quest Avid Collector.
  • Finish A Midnight Clear before completing Dead Man's Party.It fails automatically after that quest.
  • Collect your runewright upgrades as you go.
  • Make a save before the final choice with Gaunter O'Dimm.

Blood and Wine

  • Explore the Land of a Thousand Fables completely.You only visit it once. Finish the side quest Duck, Duck, Goosed and pick up everything before the story pulls you out.
  • Upgrade Corvo Bianco, your vineyard, as coin allows.
  • Collect the Grandmaster witcher gear diagrams.
  • Save before the final act — your choices decide the ending in Toussaint.
When to start the DLCs
Hearts of Stone is built for a level 30+ Geralt; Blood and Wine for level 34+. Most players do them after the main story's epilogue — the game lets you keep playing after the credits.

Chapter IX

New in Remastered

The September 2026 Remastered update is free for existing owners on PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S and Switch 2. The announced changes are about visuals, skills and gear — not the story, quests or endings, so every checklist in this guide applies.

Skill tree rework
Skills now sit in a tree with three ranks each, and some need prerequisites before they unlock. Old build guides are out of date — respec and experiment.
Reforging
Change how your gear looks without changing its stats. Visit the master craftsmen Yoana or Hattori.
Also new
Upgraded textures, lighting and animations, improved combat, an expanded Photo Mode, new accessibility options and cross-platform mod support.
