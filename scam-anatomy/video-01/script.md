Она создаёт ощущение защиты. На самом деле это подготовка ловушки. |

| EN | RU |
|---|---|
| So here are the five red flags. One: a "fraud alert" that asks you to reply. Two: a caller ID showing your bank's name. It can be faked. Three: anyone asking for a code that was texted to you. Four: any request to move your money to keep it safe. And five: pressure to do it right now. | Итак, пять красных флагов. Первый: «уведомление о мошенничестве», которое просит ответить. | And here's what to do. Don't reply to the text. Don't call any number in it. If someone calls claiming to be your bank, hang up, and call the number on the back of your card yourself.
