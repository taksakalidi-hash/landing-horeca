Step Two

What you actually need to buy

This is the most confused topic in every Forever thread right now. Here is the honest version.

The one rule that matters: when Forever launches on November 4, access is included with an active World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time. You do not need to buy any edition to play the game. The paid packs buy you two things only: the Skyborne race and beta access.

Beta reality check

The beta that opens on September 17 is capped at level 30. That shapes what it's worth to you:

Profession pairs that pay

Forever adds 600+ new recipes and, unlike Classic, gives every primary profession a perk for the character who learns it. That changes the old "gather on your main, craft on an alt" logic — a crafting profession is now worth carrying for its own passive benefit.

PairBest forWhy it works in Forever
Mining + HerbalismPure gold, any classZero material cost, and three new zones mean three fresh node populations that nobody has routed yet. Highest early-server income.
Mining + BlacksmithingMelee and tanksSelf-supplied gear through the level ranges where drops are thin, plus the profession perk on your main.
Herbalism + AlchemyRaidersConsumables are the single largest recurring raid cost. Supplying yourself from December 9 onward is worth more than the gold you'd farm instead.
Skinning + LeatherworkingSolo levelersSkinning pays you for mobs you were already killing — no route detours, which is what makes it good on a tight schedule.
Enchanting + TailoringCasters, gold on a slow burnDisenchant income scales with how much green gear the server produces, which peaks in the first two months.
Put Legacy points into the Resourcefulness tree once available — removing skill reagent requirements cuts the running cost of every crafting profession, and into Professions for milestone value. These are account-wide, so they pay out again on every alt.

Camping — the new mechanic nobody has priced yet

Forever lets you build campfires out in the world and use professions to craft buff items for everyone at the camp. It's designed to push players together socially — but it also means buff food and camp consumables become a sellable service in high-traffic leveling zones. On a fresh server in November, parking a cook at a contested crossroads is an income stream that doesn't exist in Classic. Watch beta for which items are campsite-only.

Reading the racials honestly: Skysight's +10% run speed is the strongest of the five for levelling and world PvP — movement speed compounds over hundreds of hours. Read Ley Line's regen doubles your downtime recovery, which matters most for mana classes without a pet. Wind Blessed at +1% haste is small but universal. None of them are so strong that Skyborne is mandatory — which is the point of the honest answer in Step Two.
